🌿 Bibba Plant

Medicinal Plant Information

🌱 Plant Information

Botanical Name: Semecarpus anacardium (L.f.)

Common Name: Bibba / Marking Nut

Family: Anacardiaceae

🌳 Description

Bibba is a medium-sized tree with simple leaves and small flowers. Its fruit is black when mature and contains a dark, irritating oil.

💊 Medicinal Uses

  1. Traditionally used in Ayurveda for joint problems, skin conditions and digestive complaints.
  2. The raw fruit oil can irritate the skin and is toxic if misused.
  3. Traditionally used in Ayurveda for fever management.
  4. Traditionally used in Ayurveda to support relief from swelling and inflammation.
